The Science: Poisson Goals Model

In football, goals scored follow a Poisson probability distribution. We calculate expected goals (λ) by mapping each team's custom Attack Rating against the opponent's Defense Rating, scaled against historical tournament scoring averages.

  • Dynamic Multipliers: Translates skill ratings (10-99) to Poisson coefficients.
  • Stateful Math: Pure Python simulation runs natively on our FastAPI server.
  • Host Advantage: Accounts for home-field morale boosts for hosts (USA, Canada, Mexico).

10,000 World Cups in Seconds

Predicting a single match is exciting, but predicting a tournament requires statistics. By running thousands of Monte Carlo simulations, we compile the survival rate and championship odds for every team.

  • Tie-Breaking Rules: Models group stage standings, 3rd-place wildcards, and penalty shootouts.
  • Statistical Forecasts: Compiles tournament probabilities to outline clear progression curves.
  • Instant Results: Pure Python calculations complete 1,000 simulations in under a second.

Comparing top 8 favorites

Championship Win Probability: Compares the direct percentage chance of the top 8 favorites winning the final match and lifting the trophy. Compiled by counting how many times each team won in 1,000 simulated tournaments.

Combined winning probability per group (A to L)

Odds Distribution by Group: Aggregates the combined championship winning odds of all teams in each group. This helps identify the "Group of Death" (the group with the highest concentration of winning teams).
